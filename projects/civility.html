The problem

What it addresses

Online communities need a way to review harmful content across both text and images. Civility.ai brings those inputs into a shared moderation workflow.

My contribution

What I built

Built preprocessing and classification pipelines and connected the FastAPI inference service to the React demonstration dashboard as part of Team ByteBuilders.

  • Designed the end-to-end moderation workflow from input preprocessing to classification and flag review.
  • Built preprocessing and classification pipelines for text and multimodal inputs.
  • Connected the inference API to a live demonstration dashboard used during judging.

The outcome

6th out of 240+ teams

The team placed 6th out of 240+ teams at the Agentica 2.0 offline grand finale at IIIT Sricity.

Model inference is one part of moderation: organising inputs and making flagged content reviewable completes the workflow.
