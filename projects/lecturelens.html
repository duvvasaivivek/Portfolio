The problem

What it addresses

Long lecture recordings are difficult to revisit when a student needs specific topics or a concise revision guide.

My contribution

What I built

Built the transcription and summarisation pipeline and tuned chunk overlap, temperature, and token limits.

  • Multi-stage pipeline: speech-to-text transcription, text chunking, and generative summarisation.
  • Tuned chunk overlap, temperature, and token limits against transcription fidelity.
  • Produces topic breakdowns, key formulas, and review questions from raw audio.

What the project delivers

Structured notes, key formulas, and revision questions generated from lecture audio.
