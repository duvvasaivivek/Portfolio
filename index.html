About Me

Undergrad AI & Data Science student who loves building projects

Hey! I'm Vivek, currently pursuing my B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at the Indian Institute of Information Technology, Design and Manufacturing (IIITDM), Kurnool.

I love turning ideas into working code. Over the past couple of years, I've completed internships at Infosys Springboard, IBM SkillsBuild, and Elevate Labs, where I built NLP summarizers, audio note generators, and fraud detection systems. I also actively take part in national hackathons — recently securing 6th place out of 240+ teams at Agentica 2.0 (IIIT Sricity) with our project Civility.ai.

When I'm not writing code or training models, I manage financial planning and tracking as NSS Treasurer and lead campus reading initiatives as Novella — The Reading Club Coordinator at IIITDM Kurnool.
